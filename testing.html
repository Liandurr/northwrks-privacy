Join the NORTHWRKS Android test

Help us improve NORTHWRKS before the public Google Play launch. Testers receive early access and can send honest feedback about workouts, progress tracking, partner training and the community.

What you need

  • An Android phone
  • A Google account used on that phone
  • About 10 minutes to try the main features
  • Willingness to stay opted in for 14 consecutive days

Join the tester group

Join the Google Group first:

Join NORTHWRKS Testers on Google Groups

Opt in on Google Play

After joining the group, open the Play testing link below with the same Google account. The link will be activated when the closed test is published.

Open Google Play

Tester opt-in link: The Play opt-in link will be added here once Google Play generates it.

Install and test

  1. Tap “Become a tester”.
  2. Open the Google Play download link.
  3. Install NORTHWRKS and sign in.
  4. Try starting a workout, Find Your Workout, progress, community and partner features.
  5. Stay opted in for 14 days.

Send feedback

Email feedback, bugs and suggestions to northwrks.fitness@gmail.com. Honest feedback is wanted; no positive rating or review is required.
