Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

NORTHWRKS (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a fitness and social training app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile information, such as email address, display name, profile photo and preferences.
  • Workout activity, plans, exercises, measurements and progress data that you choose to record.
  • Community content, including posts, photos, comments, likes, replies and partner connections.
  • Subscription and purchase status handled through Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Device, diagnostic and usage information needed to operate, secure and improve the app.

How we use information

We use information to provide workout tracking, recommendations, community and partner features, notifications, moderation, customer support, subscriptions, security and product improvement.

Sharing

We use service providers such as Supabase for app data and authentication, RevenueCat and Google Play for subscriptions, and other providers needed to host, secure and operate the service. We do not sell personal information.

Content you post to community or news areas may be visible to other users according to the feature’s controls. Do not post information you want to keep private.

Your choices

You can update profile information, manage visibility where controls are provided, and request account deletion from Settings. Account deletion removes your account and associated user content where our systems support that deletion. You can also contact us for privacy questions or requests.

International use

NORTHWRKS is intended for users worldwide. Privacy rights vary by location; we will handle requests according to applicable law.

Children

The service is not directed to children who are below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us.

Contact

Email northwrks.fitness@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.
