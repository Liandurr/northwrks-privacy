Delete your NORTHWRKS account

You can delete your account from inside the NORTHWRKS app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm the request.

If you cannot access the app, email northwrks.fitness@gmail.com from the email address on your account and include “Account deletion request” in the subject.

What is deleted

We delete the account, profile, workout and progress data, posts, comments, likes, partner connections and other personal data associated with the account, subject to technical processing time.

What may be retained

We may retain limited records when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or accounting purposes. Those records are retained only as long as necessary and are then deleted or anonymised.

Deleting the app does not delete your account. Use the in-app option or contact support.
